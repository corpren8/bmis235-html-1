Christmas is the best hoilday!

This is why I think so...

Christmas is SO MUCH FUN and is a holiday structured around family time and joyfulness, at least I've been lucky enough for that to be the case for myself.

The holiday season is full of candy canes, peppermint mochas, awesome decorations, and most importantly, santa claus. There is no school to worry about when its Christmas time, and if we are lucky, we get snow!

My three favorite parts about Christmas

  • So much time around all my family who I love very much!
  • Cozy fires, cute Hallmark movies, the smell of the pine tree, and the great food.
  • I love it when Santa comes.
